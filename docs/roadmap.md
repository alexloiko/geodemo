# Roadmap

## Stage 1 — MVP (done in v0.1)

- All school polyhedra with parameters, textbook (oblique) and orthographic views, rotation, zoom.
- Points on edges/segments (click with snapping, drag, exact ratio form), segments (normal /
  auxiliary), extended lines, intersection of lines.
- Plane through 3 points with automatic section polygon and labelled section points; optional
  plane extension; face highlight; ratio arcs (3x / x).
- Element list with colour, visibility, rename, delete with dependents; display modes
  All / Focus / Only selected; undo/redo.
- EN / UK interface; persistence in URL and localStorage.

## Stage 1.5 — constructions and 2D sheet (done in v0.2)

2D sheet mode; perpendicular, parallel, perpendicular bisector, angle bisector, midpoint,
intersections of lines and circles, circles (3 ways), tangents, polygons, angles with degrees,
automatic marks; the same tools inside a working plane of a solid. See
[design/constructions.md](design/constructions.md).

## Stage 2 — bodies of revolution

Cylinder, cone, truncated cone, sphere; silhouettes; elliptic sections; points on circles and
generatrices.

## Stage 3 — lessons and presentation

Ordered construction steps with playback (show the solution step by step), presentation mode,
export to PNG/SVG, a library of prepared tasks (JSON), free text notes on the canvas.

## Stage 4 — measurements and constructions

Lengths, angles between lines / line and plane / planes, distances, areas and volumes;
perpendicular to a plane, centroid, inscribed circle helpers.

## Stage 5 — reach

Touch and mobile layout, embeddable widget, optional accounts and cloud saves.
